# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good faith, respect differing viewpoints, and focus feedback on the work. Do not share private credentials, personal data, or instructions for unauthorized blockchain activity.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, malicious disclosure of secrets, and instructions or code intended to steal credentials or bypass platform security are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take any other action needed to keep the project safe. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
